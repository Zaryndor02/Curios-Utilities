Curio Utilities - Waystones integration notes

This version keeps the V9.9 Warp Stone placement unchanged:
Explorer's Compass -> Warp Stone on the side of the trousers.

Added:
- A configurable Curio Utilities key for using the Warp Stone from its Curios slot.
- Default key: G.
- The key sends a server request and asks Waystones to open its normal Warp Stone destination screen.
- The Warp Stone is taken directly from the Curios slot, so it does not need to be in the hotbar or hand.

Fixed:
- Nature's Compass key mappings were registered twice in the previous version.
- The old duplicate registration was removed. Nature's Compass, Explorer's Compass and Waystones now each register their key mappings exactly once.
- The client tick/HUD dispatch was also restored so the separated Nature's/Explorer's Compass client integrations are actually driven by the main client event handler.

Waystones remains optional.
Balm is required by Waystones itself and should be present in libs/ for local development/runtime.
